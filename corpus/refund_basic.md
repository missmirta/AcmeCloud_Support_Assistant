# Refund Policy

**Tier**: Basic  
**Last updated**: March 15, 2024

## Basic Tier Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with AcmeCloud. If you're not satisfied, here's our refund policy for Basic tier customers.

## Refund Window
Basic tier customers can request a **full refund within 7 days** of payment.

After 7 days, no refunds are available for Basic tier accounts.

## How to Request a Refund
1. Contact support@acmecloud.com
2. Include your account ID and reason for the refund
3. We'll process your request within 5-7 business days

## What's Included
- Full refund of the monthly subscription fee
- Pro-rated refunds for annual subscriptions (within 7 days of initial payment only)
- No questions asked

## What's Not Refundable
- Usage charges for API calls beyond the included amount
- Add-on services purchased separately
- Charges older than 7 days

## Processing Time
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and will appear on your original payment method.

## Cancellation vs Refund
- **Cancellation**: You can cancel anytime. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period.
- **Refund**: Only available within 7 days of payment. Cancels your account immediately.

## Questions
Contact support@acmecloud.com for refund questions.
