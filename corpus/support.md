# Support and Service Level Agreements

**Last updated**: June 5, 2024

## Support Hours

### Basic Tier Support
- **Monday - Friday, 9am - 5pm Pacific Time**
- Email support only
- Response time: within 24 business hours
- No phone support
- No after-hours or weekend support

### Enterprise Tier Support  
- **24/7 coverage** including weekends and holidays
- Priority support with faster response times
- Phone and email support
- Dedicated account manager (assigned within 5 business days of signup)
- Escalation path to engineering team

## Response Times by Priority

### Basic Tier
- All issues: 24 business hours (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm PT)

### Enterprise Tier
- **P1 (Critical)**: 1 hour, 24/7
- **P2 (High)**: 4 hours during business hours
- **P3 (Normal)**: 12 hours during business hours
- **P4 (Low)**: 48 hours

## Best Practices

1. **Use the right channel**: Email for non-urgent, phone for critical issues (Enterprise)
2. **Provide context**: Include account ID, error messages, steps to reproduce
3. **Set correct priority**: Don't mark everything as P1
4. **Check status first**: Many questions answered in knowledge base

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

### Basic Tier
- Uptime: 99.5% monthly uptime guarantee
- No financial remedies for outages
- Planned maintenance: up to 4 hours/month with 48-hour notice

### Enterprise Tier - Standard SLA
- Uptime: 99.9% monthly uptime guarantee
- Financial remedy: 10% monthly fee credit per 0.1% below SLA
- Planned maintenance: up to 2 hours/month with 7-day notice

### Enterprise Tier - Premium SLA (Optional)
- Uptime: 99.99% monthly uptime guarantee
- Financial remedy: 25% monthly fee credit per 0.01% below SLA
- Planned maintenance: coordinated with your team, off-hours only
- Additional cost: 20% of base subscription

## Questions

For questions about support or SLAs, contact your account manager (Enterprise) or support@acmecloud.com (Basic).
