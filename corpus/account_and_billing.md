# Account Management and Billing

**Last updated**: May 20, 2024

## Account Statuses

Your AcmeCloud account can have one of four statuses:

### Active
- All features and services available
- Billing is current
- No restrictions

### Trial
- 14-day evaluation period
- Limited to 100 API calls per day
- No credit card required
- All features available except:
  - Custom SLAs
  - Dedicated account manager
  - SSO integration

### Suspended
- Account temporarily disabled
- Typically due to:
  - Payment failure or overdue invoice
  - Policy violation
  - Security concern requiring investigation
- Data is preserved and accessible after reactivation
- To resolve: address the underlying issue (update payment method, contact support)

### Canceled
- Account termination requested
- Access to services ends at the end of current billing period
- Data retained for 90 days after cancellation (see Data Retention Policy)
- Can reactivate before data deletion

## Billing Cycles

### Monthly Billing
- Charges occur on the **1st of each month**
- Covers the upcoming month of service
- Pro-rated for mid-month starts (charged immediately, then normal cycle begins)

### Annual Billing
- Charges occur on the anniversary of signup
- 10% discount compared to monthly billing
- Pro-rated refunds available for downgrades

## Trial Information

### Trial Duration
- **14 days** from account creation
- Starts immediately upon signup
- No credit card required to start

### Trial Limits
- **100 API calls per day**
- **10 GB storage**
- **Standard support only** (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm PT)

### After Trial
- Choose Basic or Enterprise tier to continue
- No automatic conversion - you must manually upgrade
- Data preserved for 7 days after trial expiration

## Payment Methods

We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex)
- ACH/bank transfer (Enterprise only)
- Purchase orders (Enterprise annual contracts only)

## Billing Data Freshness

Billing and account information is typically real-time, but cached data may be up to **24 hours old** in some views. If you see a status or charge that seems incorrect, request a refresh or contact support.
