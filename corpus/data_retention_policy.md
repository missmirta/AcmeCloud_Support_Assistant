# Data Retention Policy Update

**Effective Date**: May 1, 2024

## Important Update
**This policy replaces all previous data retention policies.**

## Overview
This updated policy outlines how AcmeCloud retains customer data after account cancellation, effective May 1, 2024.

## New Retention Period
When you cancel your AcmeCloud account, we now retain your data for **90 days** after the cancellation date. This extended period gives you more time to:
- Export your data
- Change your mind and reactivate
- Retrieve any final reports or analytics
- Coordinate with your team on data migration

## What Happens After 90 Days
After the 90-day retention period:
- All customer data is permanently deleted
- Backups are purged from our systems
- Data cannot be recovered

We will send email reminders at:
- 60 days after cancellation
- 30 days before deletion
- 7 days before deletion

## Exceptions
- Legal hold requirements may extend retention
- Billing records retained for 7 years per tax regulations
- Audit logs retained for compliance purposes

## Data Export
You can export your data at any time before the end of the retention period:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Settings > Data Export
3. Download your data in JSON or CSV format

## Why We Changed This
Based on customer feedback, we extended the retention period from 30 days to 90 days to give customers more flexibility during transitions.

## Questions
For questions about data retention, contact privacy@acmecloud.com.
