# Service Level Agreement - Premium

*Effective: February 2024*

## Overview

Premium SLA is available for Enterprise tier customers at an additional **$500/month**. Provides enhanced uptime guarantees and priority support.

## Uptime Commitment

AcmeCloud guarantees **99.95% uptime** for Premium SLA customers.

### Uptime Calculation
- Measured monthly
- Excludes planned maintenance windows
- Planned maintenance announced 7 days in advance
- Maintenance windows limited to 2 hours/month

### Service Credits

If we fail to meet our uptime commitment:
- 99.9% - 99.95%: 25% monthly credit
- 99.5% - 99.9%: 50% monthly credit
- 99.0% - 99.5%: 100% monthly credit
- Below 99.0%: 200% monthly credit

Credits applied automatically to next month's bill.

## Priority Support

### Phone & Email Support
- **Critical issues**: 15-minute response, 24/7/365
- **High priority**: 1-hour response, 24/7/365
- **Medium priority**: 4-hour response
- **Low priority**: 12-hour response

### Dedicated Support
- Dedicated account manager
- Named technical account manager
- Quarterly business reviews
- Architecture consultation included

## Incident Escalation

Premium SLA customers have direct escalation path to engineering team for critical issues.

## Proactive Monitoring

- Dedicated monitoring dashboard
- Advance notification of potential issues
- Monthly health check reports
- Performance optimization recommendations

## Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

In addition to uptime, Premium SLA includes performance targets:
- API response time (p95): < 200ms
- API response time (p99): < 500ms
- Dashboard load time: < 2 seconds

## Custom SLA Terms

Premium customers may negotiate custom SLA terms for:
- Higher uptime guarantees
- Custom response time requirements
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Geographic redundancy requirements

Contact sales@acmecloud.com for custom SLA discussions.
