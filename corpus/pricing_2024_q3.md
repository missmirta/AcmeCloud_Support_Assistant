# AcmeCloud Pricing

*Published August 1, 2024*

AcmeCloud offers simple, transparent pricing for all our cloud services.

## Current Pricing Tiers

### Basic Tier
- **$15/month** per user
- 100 GB storage
- Standard support (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm PT)
- 1,000 API requests/hour
- Email support

### Enterprise Tier
- **$75/month** per user
- Unlimited storage
- Priority support (24/7)
- 10,000 API requests/hour
- Dedicated account manager
- Phone & email support
- Custom SLAs
- SSO integration

## Trial Period
All new accounts start with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

## Billing
- Billed monthly on the 1st of each month
- Pro-rated charges for mid-month upgrades
- Automatic renewal unless canceled

For questions about pricing, contact our sales team at sales@acmecloud.com.
