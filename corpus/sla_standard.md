# Service Level Agreement - Standard

*Effective: February 2024*

## Overview

This SLA applies to all Basic tier customers and covers uptime guarantees and support response times.

## Uptime Commitment

AcmeCloud guarantees **99.5% uptime** for Basic tier services.

### Uptime Calculation
- Measured monthly
- Excludes planned maintenance windows
- Planned maintenance announced 72 hours in advance

### Service Credits

If we fail to meet our uptime commitment:
- 99.0% - 99.5%: 10% monthly credit
- 95.0% - 99.0%: 25% monthly credit
- Below 95.0%: 50% monthly credit

Credits applied automatically to next month's bill.

## Support Response Times

### Email Support
- **Initial response**: Within 24 business hours
- **Business hours**: Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm Pacific Time
- **Holidays**: Extended response times may apply

### Support Channels
- Email: support@acmecloud.com
- Knowledge base: docs.acmecloud.com
- Community forum: community.acmecloud.com

## Incident Priority Levels

1. **Critical**: Service completely unavailable - 24 hour response
2. **High**: Major functionality impaired - 24 hour response
3. **Medium**: Minor functionality issue - 48 hour response
4. **Low**: General questions, feature requests - 72 hour response

## Exclusions

This SLA does not cover:
- Issues caused by customer misconfiguration
- Third-party service failures
- Network issues outside AcmeCloud infrastructure
- Beta or preview features
- Scheduled maintenance windows

## Requesting Service Credits

To request an SLA credit:
1. Submit ticket within 30 days of incident
2. Include affected time period
3. Provide error logs or screenshots
4. Credits processed within 1 billing cycle
