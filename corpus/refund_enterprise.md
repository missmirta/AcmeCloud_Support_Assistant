# Refund Policy - Enterprise

**Tier**: Enterprise  
**Last updated**: March 15, 2024

## Enterprise Tier Refund Policy

We value our Enterprise customers and offer an extended refund policy to ensure your satisfaction.

## Refund Window
Enterprise tier customers can request a **full refund within 30 days** of payment.

This extended window gives you time to:
- Fully evaluate the platform
- Test integrations
- Onboard your team
- Ensure AcmeCloud meets your needs

## How to Request a Refund
1. Contact your dedicated account manager, or
2. Email enterprise-support@acmecloud.com
3. Include your account ID and reason for the refund
4. We'll process your request within 3-5 business days

## What's Included
- Full refund of the monthly or annual subscription fee
- Pro-rated refunds for annual subscriptions (within 30 days of initial payment)
- No questions asked within the 30-day window

## What's Not Refundable
- Professional services fees (training, custom development)
- Usage charges beyond the included amount
- Add-on services purchased separately

## Processing Time
Enterprise refunds are prioritized and processed within 3-5 business days. Refunds will appear on your original payment method.

## Partial Refunds
After the 30-day window, Enterprise customers may still request pro-rated refunds in special circumstances. Contact your account manager to discuss options.

## Cancellation vs Refund
- **Cancellation**: You can cancel anytime. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period, and you retain access to all features.
- **Refund**: Available within 30 days of payment. Cancels your account immediately upon refund processing.

## Questions
Contact your dedicated account manager or enterprise-support@acmecloud.com for refund questions.
